I’m a fifth-year Computer Science PhD student at Oregon State University. I study machine learning, focusing on applications in computational sustainability. I’m also interested in intuitive and engaging ways of learning about machine learning. Previously, I was an undergraduate student of Computer Science and Engineering, and then a research assistant at North South University.
